# Space ID — Core Usage Model

## 1. Camera is the primary user interface

The camera is the main way users interact with Space ID. Users should not be trained to open the app merely to browse advertising.

Primary flow:

**Open camera → point at something → Space ID resolves the relevant Space → show the Space's current result.**

The result may be an offer, an eligible advertisement, a public identity, a commercial listing, or another Space-specific experience.

## 2. Browsing advertising is optional

A user who wants to browse offers and advertisements without pointing the camera can use the application's menu.

The menu provides categorized browsing, for example:

- Offers
- Restaurants / food offers
- Retail offers
- Real estate
- Cars
- Events
- Services
- Other advertising categories

The category structure is a discovery/browsing interface. It does not replace the camera as the primary Space ID interaction.

## 3. Space owner offer is limited to one active offer

A merchant such as a restaurant or shop can create its own Space ID and publish **one active offer at a time**.

The merchant does not need to maintain a menu, full price list, booking system, catalog, or general business profile inside Space ID merely to use the advertising service.

Example:

> Restaurant Space → "Burger meal for AED 25"

The merchant can replace that offer later, but it does not publish multiple simultaneous owner offers through the same owner-offer slot.

## 4. Owner offer and platform advertising inventory are different

The owner's single offer is separate from the platform's advertising inventory.

A Space can therefore have:

- one active owner offer; and
- eligibility to receive multiple external campaigns over time, selected by the platform's advertising decision engine.

This preserves the multi-campaign advertising model without forcing merchants to manage a complex content system.

## 5. Product principle

Space ID should create utility first and advertising access second:

**Camera discovery is the primary behavior.**

**Menu-based advertising discovery is the secondary behavior.**

**Advertising is a monetization layer over the Space network, not the reason the user must open the camera.**
