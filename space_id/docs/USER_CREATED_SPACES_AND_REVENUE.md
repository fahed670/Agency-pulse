# Space ID — User-Created Spaces & Revenue Model

This document is a core product rule, not an optional monetization feature.

## 1. Who creates the advertising inventory?

Space ID does not centrally create the physical advertising inventory.

Users create Spaces themselves using the mobile application. A user can identify and register an eligible physical or geographic area as a Space ID.

The platform provides the identity, validation, delivery, measurement and commercial infrastructure around that user-created Space.

## 2. Space types

A user may create at least these forms of Space:

### A. Flat surface Space

Examples:
- wall
- floor
- window
- facade
- other suitable physical surface

The Space represents a defined physical surface or area.

### B. Geographic / open-area Space

A Space can represent a geographic area without a physical flat surface.

Examples:
- an open area
- a pedestrian area
- a defined location
- a route or passage context where the product rules permit discovery

The digital content becomes discoverable when users physically enter, pass through, or otherwise qualify for that geographic Space.

## 3. Annual Space fee

Each user-created advertising Space costs **AED 5 per year**.

The fee is attached to the Space itself, not to each advertisement or campaign shown within it.

The Space must have an active paid term before it can participate in the advertising inventory.

## 4. Revenue entitlement

For an eligible advertising Space created and owned/controlled by a user:

- Year 1: **70% of eligible advertising revenue generated by that Space goes to the Space owner.**
- Platform share in Year 1: **30%**.
- From Year 2 onward, if the owner renews the Space: **30% of eligible advertising revenue goes to the Space owner**, with the remaining **70% belonging to the platform** unless another commercial rule is explicitly introduced.

The entitlement is calculated from qualified, billable advertising activity attributable to the Space, not from raw camera opens or raw recognition events.

## 5. Multiple campaigns per Space

A Space is advertising inventory, not a single advertisement.

One active Space can simultaneously qualify for campaigns from:
- restaurants
- banks
- real estate sellers/lessors
- retail businesses
- events
- vehicle sellers
- other eligible categories

The decision engine selects an eligible campaign for each valid advertising opportunity.

## 6. Other commercial uses

Space ID is not limited to advertising.

The same identity infrastructure can support discoverable commercial listings, including:
- property for sale
- property for rent
- vehicles for sale
- businesses
- services
- public or creator profiles
- other user-authorized offerings

The product must distinguish advertising impressions from marketplace/listing discovery and transaction events for accounting and analytics.

## 7. Physical presence matters

A geographic Space is not a generic web banner. Its value depends on physical presence or another explicitly supported qualification signal.

A person should not be credited with an advertising impression merely because the Space record was opened remotely.

The system must record the evidence used to qualify an interaction and apply the product's eligibility rules before it becomes billable.

## 8. Anti-abuse principle

Opening the camera, recognizing a Space, or requesting its content does not automatically create a billable impression.

The billing pipeline must use a separate qualified-impression record with controls such as:
- frequency limits
- cooldowns
- duplicate-event protection
- session controls
- velocity/anomaly detection
- server-side validation

## 9. Product philosophy

The network grows because users create inventory themselves.

The platform therefore acts as the infrastructure layer that:
1. identifies the physical/geographic Space;
2. validates and manages its lifecycle;
3. makes the Space discoverable;
4. supplies content and campaigns;
5. measures qualified interactions;
6. calculates the owner's entitlement;
7. enables the owner to earn from the Space.

This is a foundational product rule and must be reflected in the database, APIs, mobile flows, billing ledger, ownership model and advertising decision engine.
